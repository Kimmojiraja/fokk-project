Sustaining Independent Privacy Software

Support Fokk's Independence

Fokk Browser has zero ads, zero trackers, and zero telemetry. It is built and maintained by a small team of privacy advocates. Without community support, independent privacy software cannot survive. Here's exactly how we sustain the project — transparently.

Our Commitment to You
We will never sell your data, insert advertising, enable tracking SDKs, or accept revenue from surveillance-based businesses. All revenue models listed here are opt-in, transparent, and do not compromise your privacy in any way. The free tier of Fokk Browser remains genuinely free, forever — with zero feature restrictions on core browsing.

How We're Funded
Sustainable Without Compromising Users
Unlike ad-funded browsers, Fokk uses only privacy-respecting revenue streams. No data is sold, no profiles are built, no ads are displayed.
GitHub Sponsors & Open Collective
Individual and corporate sponsorships through GitHub Sponsors. All sponsors are listed publicly. No corporate sponsor gains influence over product decisions.
Fokk Pro & Teams Subscriptions
Optional paid tiers providing power-user features. Subscription revenue directly funds developer salaries, server costs, and security audits.
Privacy-Respecting Search Revenue
Optional rev-share partnerships with Kagi, Brave Search, and DuckDuckGo — only if you choose them as your default search engine. Zero data is shared with these partners beyond the search query you type.
Open Source Grants
Applications to open-source sustainability funds (NLnet Foundation, Mozilla Open Source Support, Sovereign Tech Fund) to fund long-term security and infrastructure work.
