Privacy Policy

Fokk Browser collects no personal data, sends no telemetry, and communicates with no advertising networks. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data — and what doesn't.

Last updated: November 2026 · Version 1.1 · Applies to Fokk Browser v1.1.0+
The Short Version
Zero Data Collection — The Absolute Guarantee
Fokk Browser does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data, browsing history, search queries, URLs, keystrokes, screenshots, or device identifiers to any external party. All browsing data stays on your device and in your control.

Third-Party Network Contacts

Fokk Browser does contact external services on your behalf, but only when you explicitly trigger actions that require them:

  • Your chosen search engine — receives your search query when you submit a search.
  • Websites you navigate to — standard HTTPS connections to sites you visit.
  • DNS-over-HTTPS resolver — your DNS queries go through the DoH provider you select (Cloudflare, Quad9, Mullvad). Fokk does not receive these; they go directly from your device to the resolver.
  • Google S2 Favicons API — domain names (never full URLs) are sent to fetch favicon images for shortcuts and bookmarks. No personal data accompanies these requests.
  • RSS news feeds — if you use the news briefing section, your IP connects to public RSS endpoints. No personal data is sent beyond what a standard browser request includes.
  • GitHub Releases API — Fokk checks for updates only when you manually click "Check for Updates." No automatic background calls are made.

All other network requests from Fokk itself are either blocked (by the ad-blocking engine) or do not exist. Fokk contains no background telemetry scheduler, no heartbeat ping, and no analytics SDK.

Incognito / Stealth Mode

When browsing in Incognito mode:

  • No browsing history is written to localStorage or any file.
  • Cookies are stored only in memory and are discarded when the incognito session ends.
  • No cache files are written to disk.
  • Web storage (localStorage, IndexedDB, sessionStorage) is isolated and cleared on session end.
  • The news briefing and shortcut sections are hidden to avoid information leakage into the UI.

Important limitation: Incognito mode does not hide your IP address from the websites you visit or your ISP. Use the VPN/DoH features for additional network-level privacy.

Disclaimers & Limitations
VPN Feature — Important Limitations
The VPN/tunnel feature in Fokk routes your traffic through a privacy relay but is NOT a fully anonymous Tor-equivalent solution. It reduces ISP visibility and masks your IP from websites, but does not provide the same anonymity guarantees as Tor. For extreme privacy scenarios (journalists, activists), supplement with dedicated Tor Browser or Tails OS.
Fokk Is Not Legal Counsel
Using a privacy browser does not make illegal activities legal. Fokk does not provide legal advice. Users are responsible for complying with the laws of their jurisdiction when browsing online.
No Guarantee of Perfect Security
No software can guarantee 100% security. Zero-day vulnerabilities in Chromium, Electron, or the operating system could expose you even when using Fokk. Keep your OS and Fokk updated to minimize risk. Report security vulnerabilities privately to us before public disclosure.
Your Rights
Right to Delete
Go to Settings → Privacy Vault → Purge Data to delete all locally stored history, cookies, and cache at any time. No confirmation required, no waiting period.
Right to Export
Your bookmarks and shortcuts are stored in browser localStorage as plain JSON. You can read and export them at any time using browser DevTools (Ctrl+Shift+I).
Right to Know
Our full source code is public on GitHub. You can audit every network call, every data storage operation, and every privacy-sensitive function. No black boxes.
Right to Report
If you discover that Fokk Browser is collecting data not described in this policy, please report it immediately on GitHub Issues or via our security contact. We take such reports seriously and will respond within 72 hours.
