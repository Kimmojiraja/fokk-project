Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Fokk Browser? Everything you need to know about our privacy architecture, performance, and features.

How does Fokk block YouTube ads without speed slowdowns or delays?
Unlike browser extensions that artificially accelerate video playback to 16x speed or trigger YouTube anti-adblock warnings, Fokk strips ad payloads directly at document-start before the player initializes. Videos play smoothly at native 1x speed with zero lag, zero black frames, and zero warnings.
How does the built-in WebTorrent streamer work?
Fokk has a built-in WebTorrent engine running over WebRTC. When you open a magnet link or .torrent file, Fokk begins downloading video chunks sequentially and pipes them into an HTML5 video stream in real-time, allowing instant playback without installing external apps like qBittorrent or waiting hours for full file completion.
What is the difference between Dual Split and Quad 4-Grid mode?
Dual Split View divides your browser window into two independent side-by-side viewports with a draggable divider and independent address bars. Quad 4-Grid mode arranges four fully functional webviews into a 2x2 grid layout, ideal for financial traders, software engineers, and researchers monitoring multiple live streams simultaneously.
Does Fokk collect any telemetry, search queries, or browsing history?
No. Fokk has zero telemetry servers, zero user analytics, and zero tracking pings. Your browsing history, bookmarks, and sessions remain strictly encrypted on your local machine. You can also use the 1-click Burn Session tool to immediately purge all browsing artifacts at any time.
How does the AI Paywall Defuser work?
When you encounter a paywalled news article, the AI Defuser banner automatically queries verified public archive mirrors and serves a clean, readable version. Combined with our high-contrast Reader View, it strips clutter, sticky subscription banners, and paywall overlays.
Can I use standard Google sign-in and Passkeys?
Yes. Fokk is built on Chromium and Electron 34, offering complete compatibility with Google Accounts, 2-Step Verification, WebAuthn Passkeys, and modern web applications.
Is Fokk Browser really 100% free? How is it funded?
Yes! Fokk Browser is completely free and open-source under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3). It is funded solely by community sponsors, GitHub sponsors, and privacy advocates who believe the web should be free from corporate surveillance.
