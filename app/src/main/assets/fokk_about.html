Core Capabilities

Client-Side Engineering by Design

Every component operates with low latency, minimal memory overhead, and zero analytical profiling. All filtering and routing execution happens locally on your hardware.

AdShield Filtering Engine
Intercepts network connections and player payloads at document start, excising ad sequences and tracking beacons before web players initialize—without artificial playback fast-forwarding or broken layouts.
Fokk Relay (Oblivious Split-Trust)
Decouples source user identity from destination traffic via cryptographic multi-hop nodes. Injects packet timing micro-jitter into active sessions to actively defeat deep packet inspection and traffic fingerprinting.
Fokk AI Truth Engine
Real-time forensic verification, bias auditing, and community fact-checking directly over social media feeds (X, Reddit, Threads, YouTube). Operates with privacy-preserving local heuristics.
Native Media Hub & WebTorrent
Streams audio and video directly in your browser tabs from magnet links and .torrent files over native WebRTC without external apps, with continuous background playback.
Isolated Private Sandboxing
Private sessions run in a segregated operating system process and directory (fokk_private). Cookies, storage caches, and history are completely purged upon closing the session.
